Privacy Policy

Neon Word Game  ·  com.techbro.wordsearch

Effective date: 21 October 2026

Developer: TechBro

Contact: privacy@yourdomain.com

This policy explains how Neon Word Game ("the App"), an Android word-search puzzle game, handles information when you use it on an Android device. The App is developed and published by TechBro ("we", "us", "our"). We are the data controller for the limited processing described here.

Summary of the essentials

  • We do not ask for an account, your name, or your email address.
  • Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The App is advertised by Google AdMob, and third-party vendors may use device identifiers for ad purposes.
  • We use Firebase Analytics to measure app performance and reliability.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings or through Google's Ads settings.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Neon Word Game mobile application distributed through Google Play. It does not apply to other websites or applications operated by TechBro, nor to third-party services that operate independently of us, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

2. Information we collect

We collect as little as reasonably possible. The App does not require registration and does not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

2.1 Information stored only on your device

To make the game work, the App stores the following data locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to us and we cannot access it:

Stored dataPurpose
Level and puzzle completion records, stars and best scoresSaving your progress across sessions
Hint balance and in-game coin totalManaging hint and reward economy
Settings: music, sound, haptic feedback, volume, reduce-motion preferenceRemembering your preferences
Record of which facts you have already viewedNot repeating facts unnecessarily

This data is stored using Android application storage (SharedPreferences and app-private files) and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage. It is never uploaded to a server controlled by us.

2.2 Advertising identifiers and advertising data

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and its advertising partners may process:

  • Your Android advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • Information about your device, such as model, manufacturer, operating system and version, screen resolution, and locale
  • Your approximate location derived from your IP address (at country/region level)
  • Your IP address and network status
  • Advertising interaction data, such as whether an ad was requested, displayed, viewed, or clicked
  • Inferred interests derived from your app activity, used to select which ads to show you

You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID at any time via Settings → Privacy → Ads on your device. You can opt out of personalised advertising with participating advertisers at google.com/settings/ads, or viaoptout.aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.eu.

2.3 Analytics and crash data

The App uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used and to detect stability problems. This may include:

  • App version, device model, and operating system version
  • Approximate country and language settings
  • Gameplay events, such as which difficulty or level pack is played and whether a puzzle is completed
  • Crash and error diagnostics, including the stack trace and device state at the time of the error

Analytics events describegameplay, not your identity. We do not use them to build a profile of you.

2.4 Ad personalisation and users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

For users located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google requires advertising consent before personalised ads may be served.

Current implementation. At the time this policy was published, this version of the App uses Google AdMob with automated ad delivery. Advertisers may use your device identifier and inferred interests to serve personalised, interest-based advertising. A dedicated in-app consent form has not yet been implemented in this version, so personalised advertising may be delivered without a prior in-app consent prompt in these regions.

We are updating the App to introduce a consent flow via Google's User Messaging Platform, after which advertising will default to non-personalised and you will be asked to opt in to personalised ads. We will update this section and publish a new version of this policy when that change ships.

Regardless of region, you can opt out of personalised ads using the links in section 2.2, and you can opt out of a specific advertiser by visiting its opt-out page, which is required for certain advertising networks participating in Google's ad ecosystem.

2.5 Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions, and never collects personal information through them:

PermissionWhy it is used
INTERNET Requested by the AdMob and Firebase SDKs to load ads and transmit analytics. The App has no server connection of its own.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used by the AdMob SDK to check connectivity before requesting ads, avoiding failed ad loads.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Used to play background music smoothly while you are playing the game.

The App requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, or storage permission.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Display advertisements and provide optional rewarded-video bonuses (extra hints or additional time)
  • Measure app performance, diagnose crashes, and improve the game
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising outside of the advertising partners described in section 2.2, who act as independent controllers for the data they receive.

4. Advertising and rewarded video

The App showsrewarded video advertisements that you choose to watch. Watching an advertisement may grant you an in-game hint or additional puzzle time. Advertisements are entirely optional: the App is fully playable without watching any ad, and no gameplay feature is ever locked behind a purchase. There is no in-app purchase, subscription, or paid removal of ads.

5. Data retention

  • Game progress and settings: retained on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its storage.
  • Analytics data: retained by Google in line with the Firebase Analytics data retention settings, and deleted automatically after a limited period (default maximum of 14 months) unless configured otherwise.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google and its partners according to their own privacy policies.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or under16 where that is the applicable age of digital consent). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Because the App displays advertising and is not designed for children, it is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families programme. Please do not allow children under 13 to use the App. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information through the App, contact us at privacy@yourdomain.com and we will investigate and delete it.

8. Legal bases for processing (GDPR/UK GDPR)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following bases:

  • Consent — for personalised advertising and any optional data use where you are asked for consent.
  • Legitimate interests — for securing the App, understanding aggregate usage, and improving gameplay, balanced against your rights.
  • Performance of a contract — for processing strictly necessary to run the App on your device.
  • Legal obligation — where retention or disclosure is required by law.

Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it at any time through the Google Ads settings linked in section 2.2, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export your personal data, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

Because game progress is stored only on your device, you can exercise these rights over that data simply by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it. For data held by Google as an independent controller, please use Google's own tools, such as Google My Account andGoogle Ads Settings, or contact Google directly.

To make a request regarding data associated with this App, contact us at privacy@yourdomain.com. We will respond within a reasonable period, and within 30 days where required by law.

10. International transfers

Google processes data on servers located in the United States and other countries. Where data is transferred outside the EEA or UK, Google relies on Standard Contractual Clauses and other transfer mechanisms approved by the European Commission. You can learn more at Google's privacy policy.

11. Security

We do not transmit your game progress to our servers, because we never receive it. Data processed by Google is protected by Google's own security measures and encryption. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to this policy

The "Effective date" at the top of this page always reflects the current version. Material changes will be noted in the App's release notes on Google Play.
